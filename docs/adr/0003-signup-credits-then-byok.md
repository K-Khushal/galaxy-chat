# One-time sign-up Credits, then BYOK, all through OpenRouter

Sign-up is open to anyone, so every new account costs the operator money. Each User gets 10 Credits once, at sign-up. A credit pays for one Reply on a short list of cheap Credit models, served with the operator's Platform key. Credits never renew, and there are no daily or monthly caps, credit ledgers or budget plans. After the Credits are gone, or to use any other model, the User saves their own OpenRouter BYOK key. OpenRouter serves both paths, so one key unlocks every provider. This replaces the inherited credit-reservation ledger, $10/month platform ceiling and guest flow on purpose; don't reintroduce recurring allowances without revisiting this decision.

## Consequences

- To stop users deleting their account and signing up again for fresh Credits, a hashed record of verified emails and GitHub/Google account IDs that already received Credits survives account deletion. A returning identity gets an account with 0 Credits. The privacy page must say so.
